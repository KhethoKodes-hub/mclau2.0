About McLau Solutions

Proudly South African. Committed to Excellence.

Founded in 2019, McLau Solutions (Pty) Ltd is a 100% Youth, Women, and Black-owned company based in Kathu, Northern Cape. We specialise in the manufacturing and supply of high-quality detergents, cleaning materials, and consumables.

Our products are tested in collaboration with the Tshwane University of Technology and the Technology Innovation Agency, ensuring full SABS compliance. We operate across the Northern Cape, with an extended footprint in Gauteng through strategic partnerships.
