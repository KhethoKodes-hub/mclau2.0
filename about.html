About McLau Solutions (Pty) Ltd

Proudly South African. Built on Quality & Purpose.

Founded in 2019, McLau Solutions (Pty) Ltd is a 100% Youth, Women, and Black-owned company specialising in the manufacturing and supply of high-quality detergents, cleaning materials, and consumables. Based in Kathu, Northern Cape, we are dedicated to creating innovative and effective cleaning solutions that meet the highest industry standards.

Our manufacturing facility ensures quality control from production to delivery, with all products tested in collaboration with the Tshwane University of Technology and the Technology Innovation Agency, ensuring full compliance with SABS standards.

We operate across the Northern Cape, with an extended footprint in Gauteng through strategic partnerships — proud to serve both private and corporate sectors in the John Taolo Gaetsewe District and beyond.
