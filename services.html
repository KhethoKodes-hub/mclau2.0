Cleaning Services

Professional cleaning for households, offices, and industrial facilities — using our own SABS-compliant products throughout.

Household & Office Cleaning

Thorough, reliable cleaning for homes and office environments. We handle everything from routine maintenance cleans to deep cleans.

  • Reception and common area cleaning
  • Workstation and surface sanitisation
  • Waste removal and restocking
  • Scheduled or once-off bookings

Industrial Cleaning

Large-scale industrial cleaning for warehouses, factories, mining facilities, and commercial sites across the Northern Cape.

  • High-pressure surface cleaning
  • Floor scrubbing and degreasing
  • Equipment exterior cleaning
  • Post-construction clean-ups

Carpet & Furniture Cleaning

Specialised deep cleaning for carpets, chairs, sofas, and other upholstered furniture in office and commercial settings.

  • Hot water extraction carpet cleaning
  • Chair and upholstery steam cleaning
  • Stain and odour treatment
  • PPE cleaning and decontamination

Kitchen & Bathroom Maintenance

Hygienic deep cleaning of kitchen and bathroom facilities for commercial, industrial, and residential clients.

  • Appliance and surface degreasing
  • Grout, tile, and basin scrubbing
  • Drain cleaning and deodorising
  • Restroom hygiene restocking

PPE & Equipment Cleaning

Proper cleaning and decontamination of personal protective equipment to comply with health and safety regulations.

  • Helmets, gloves, and vests
  • Safety boots and overalls
  • Decontamination on-site
  • Compliant with HSE standards
